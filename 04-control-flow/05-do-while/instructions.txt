TOPIC: Do-While Loops

OBJECTIVE
Understand loops that must execute their body at least once.

CONCEPTS
- do-while
- Condition checked after execution
- Difference between while and do-while

PRACTICE
1. Create a basic do-while loop.
2. Use a condition that is initially true.
3. Use a condition that is initially false.
4. Compare the same condition using while and do-while.

CHALLENGE
Demonstrate a situation where while executes zero times but do-while executes once.
