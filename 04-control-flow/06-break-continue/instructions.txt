TOPIC: Break and Continue

OBJECTIVE
Learn how to alter the normal flow of a loop.

CONCEPTS
- break
- continue
- Exiting a loop
- Skipping an iteration

PRACTICE
1. Use break to stop a loop early.
2. Use continue to skip an iteration.
3. Observe the difference between break and continue.
4. Use them inside for and while loops.

CHALLENGE
Create a loop that skips specific values and stops completely at another value.
