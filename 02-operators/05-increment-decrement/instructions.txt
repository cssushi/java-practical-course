TOPIC: Increment and Decrement

OBJECTIVE
Understand how ++ and -- modify a variable by one.

CONCEPTS
- ++ Increment
- -- Decrement
- Prefix increment/decrement
- Postfix increment/decrement
- Evaluation order

PRACTICE
1. Use ++ and -- as standalone statements.
2. Compare ++x with x++.
3. Compare --x with x--.
4. Observe how prefix and postfix forms behave inside expressions.

CHALLENGE
Predict the output of expressions involving prefix and postfix operators before running them.
